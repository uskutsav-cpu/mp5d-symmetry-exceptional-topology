# Scientific scope

## Question

Does the enhanced `U(2)` symmetry of the equal-spin surface `δ = 0` organize
degeneracies, symmetry multiplets, exceptional lines, exceptional-chain
junctions, or higher-order exceptional points in the massive-scalar QNM spectrum
of the asymptotically flat doubly rotating 5D Myers-Perry black hole, and how do
these structures unfold for `δ ≠ 0`?

## In scope

* The exact representation-theoretic structure of the equal-spin spectrum.
* Which degeneracies are symmetry-protected and which are accidental.
* Whether accidental within-sector collisions become defective.
* The topology of any exceptional set in `(s, δ, μ)`.
* Effective local models constrained by the symmetry.
* Rigorous negative / no-go statements, which are an acceptable outcome.
* Computer-assisted certification methodology.

## Out of scope (this phase)

Manuscript, publication figures, cover letter, journal formatting, outreach.
