# Prior results — what must NOT be presented as new

Bibliographic metadata for every entry is to be verified before it is cited;
see `bibliography/mandatory_sources.csv` for verification status. **No entry has
been verified yet in this session** — the corpus is indexed, not checked.

Already established, and out of scope as novelty claims:

* massive-scalar QNMs of singly rotating 5D Myers-Perry;
* massive-scalar QNMs of doubly rotating 5D Myers-Perry (Huang & Huang,
  arXiv:2502.11764);
* long-lived / quasi-bound scalar modes in those systems;
* exceptional points and EP cascades in massive-scalar Kerr;
* eigenvalue repulsion in Kerr-Newman and rotating de Sitter black holes;
* exceptional lines in modified Regge-Wheeler and near-Nariai systems;
* square-root branching at ordinary second-order EPs;
* `ε^{1/q}` pseudospectral scaling near an order-`q` Jordan block;
* separability of the massive scalar on Myers-Perry (Murata & Soda;
  Vasudevan, Stevens & Page). **This project re-derives the separation only to
  fix conventions and to make them machine-checkable, not as a new result.**

The project's question is specifically the *organizing role of the equal-spin
U(2) symmetry in the exceptional-set topology*, which is not addressed by any of
the above.
